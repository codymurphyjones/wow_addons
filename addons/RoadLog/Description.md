# RoadLog

**Map Azeroth's roads by walking them. RoadLog records your path while it's on and draws every road you've logged on the world map.**

---

## Features

### One click to record
Click the minimap button when you step onto a road and walk down the middle of it. Click again when you leave it. While it's on, every step counts as road.

### Pauses by itself
Recording pauses while you're dead, swimming, flying, on a flight path, indoors or in an instance, and picks up again once you're back on the road. Hearthstones and portals never draw a line across the map.

### See what you've covered
Recorded roads are drawn on the world map, and the stretch you're walking grows as you go.

### Small, clean data
Points every 4 yards on curves, and none in between on straight stretches. Everything is stored in world coordinates and shared by all your characters.

## Commands
- `/roadlog`: turn recording on or off
- `/roadlog map`: show or hide the roads on the map
- `/roadlog undo`: delete the last stretch
- `/roadlog stats`: how much road you've recorded
