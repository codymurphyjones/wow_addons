Scrying Goggles? No Problem!

Find Narain's Scrying Goggles and return them to Narain Soothfancy in Tanaris.
Narain's Scrying Goggles (1)

Description

<You hear a sigh of relief escape the crate.>

You just want his scrying goggles? Great! That will be no problem... Just give me a minute to... Hey! Where the... Dagnabbit!

Well, there's some bad news and some worse news. The bad news is that I lost the goggles. The worse news is that I lost them when I was nosing around in the Molten Core last month. Yep, the Molten Core... I guess that concludes our business! Bye!
