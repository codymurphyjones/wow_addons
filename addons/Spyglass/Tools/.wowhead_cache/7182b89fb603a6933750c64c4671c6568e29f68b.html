Delivery to Ridgewell

Bring Ridgewell's Crate to Remington Ridgewell in Stormwind.
Ridgewell's Crate (Provided) (1)

Description

I have packaged Doomriggers Clasp into a crate, suitable for shipment to Stormwind. <name>, you have been extremely helpful -- can I ask you for one last favor?

Deliver the crate to Count Remington Ridgewell. You will find him in the Keep of Stormwind. I am sure he'll be very pleased to see what you have for him.

Progress

I was told a messenger would come.  One with a gift from Blackrock Spire.

Are you he?

Completion

Ah, so you are he!  You have my thanks, <name>.  And... if you didn't already know... the gratitude of a noble in the house of Ridgewell is nigh worth a king's ransom.
