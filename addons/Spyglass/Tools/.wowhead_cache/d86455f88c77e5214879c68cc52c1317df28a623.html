Superior Armaments of Battle - Exalted Amongst the Dawn

For 6 Insignias of the Dawn and 6 Insignias of the Crusade you may choose an item from the Argent Dawn's treasure cache.
Insignia of the Dawn (6)
Insignia of the Crusade (6)

Description

Greetings, friend. Have you insignias of the Dawn or the Crusade that you would like to redeem for items of power?

Items offered to those exalted amongst the Dawn are discounted. I encourage you to make a purchase!

Progress

Choose carefully. There are no refunds.

Completion

Rewards

You will be able to choose one of these rewards:
Band of Piety Band of Resolution
Verimonde's Last Resort Supply Bag
Leggings of the Plague Hunter Sanctified Leather Helm

Gains

  • 100 reputation with Argent Dawn

The Wowhead Client is a little application we use to keep our database up to date, and to provide you with some nifty extra functionality on the website!
